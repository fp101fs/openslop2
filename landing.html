A StarCraft II style RTS for the browser: the Wings of Liberty and Nova Covert Ops campaigns, skirmish against the AI, and online games with friends. Play it in third person, first person or the classic top-down view.

It is an unofficial fan project made for fun. Everyone gets the same game, with no microtransactions or paid perks.

Bugs and questions: GitHub issues.

Source code on GitHub. Built with three.js and Vite; online play uses PeerJS.

StarCraft is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment. OpenSlop II is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard.

Campaigns

Wings of Liberty (29 missions) and Nova Covert Ops are the main paths, with OpenSlop II Originals on the side. Progress is saved in this browser.

Skirmish

Pick a race and a map and fight the AI at the difficulty you choose.

Host a game and share the 5-letter room code, join with a friend's code, or use Find Game to be matched with an open game. 1v1 and 2-player co-op against the AI.
