# Wings of Liberty — mission spec (Liquipedia, read in Chrome 2026-11-07; paraphrased). Flow/unlock rules: wings-of-liberty-flow.md
Numbers marked (?) = our estimate. Each mission implemented in src/campaign/campaigns/wol/missions/<name>.ts.

## 1 Liberation Day — "Destroy the Dominion Outpost"
Full research: [wol/01-liberation-day/README.md](wol/01-liberation-day/README.md).
Primary: destroy the Logistics HQ; Raynor must survive. Bonus: destroy 6 Dominion Holoboards. Start: Raynor + 5 Marines, a road to the HQ. Entering the square plaza: 3 drop pods deliver extra Marines (about 9 total) once the dialog starts. West/north to a bridge: kill the guard to free civilians who join and throw molotovs; a Dominion transporter nearby. Past the bridge there is no return; final Vikings land to guard the HQ. Raynor has +1 range vs Marines. Achievements: Raynor kills 5 enemies (Normal), kill every enemy (Hard), all 6 holoboards in < 3 min (Normal).
## 2 The Outlaws — "Seize the Xel'Naga Artifact" (credits 45,000)
Primary: destroy the Dominion base (digging site). Bonus: rescue the outlaw base to the west by reaching its beacon (they hold Marines, Medics, a Barracks, depots, resources) — it is under repeated Dominion marine attack. Start: small base, SCVs, Barracks; build Marines/Medics (Medic 75/50, needs Tech Lab). Resource pallets across the map. Enemy base north: Factory/Hellions, bunker, a sieged tank on Brutal. Achievements: collect all pallets (Normal), < 10 min (Hard), no extra Barracks (Normal).
## 3 Zero Hour — "Hold out for Evacuation (20 minutes)" (credits 55,000)
Primary: survive 20:00; when the countdown reaches 1 minute zerg send unending waves; waves strengthen over time; Banelings appear. Unlocks Bunker + Missile Turret. Start: lower-right established base, 4 bunkers (2 atop the ramp, 2 at the bridges). Bonus: rescue 3 rebel squads (east, centre, west; revealed one at a time at about 16:30, 12:00, 7:30 per earlier guide) — each guarded by burrowed zerglings/hydralisks. Hold-out lose condition: all structures destroyed. 8 hatcheries on the map (2 NW, 3 NE, 3 SW); the zerg rebuild destroyed ones. Hard/Brutal: zerg drop pods deliver creep tumors + zerglings, nearing the base over time. Achievements: lose no structure (Normal); 4 hatcheries (Hard); 8 hatcheries (Normal).
## 4 The Evacuation — "Evacuate the Agria Colony" (credits 100,000, 3 Zerg research)
Primary: reach Dr. Hanson's settlement (no-build start segment with 2 Firebats + 2 Medics, collect containers); escort 50 colonists to the colony ships via convoys of APCs/transport trucks along a road with 3 entrances (zerg come from the side paths, none on the road); then you build a base at the settlement. Later waves include drop pods and Nydus worms; wave size scales with colonists evacuated. Bonus: collect 3 Chrysalis DNA (+1 Zerg research each) — near the spaceport ramp, middle-left, right of the road midpoint; lightly guarded by burrowed units. Two zerg bases (NW corner, mid-right). Achievements: lose no transport truck (Normal); lose/salvage no structure (Hard); destroy all zerg structures (Normal).
## 5 Outbreak — "Investigate the Meinhoff infestation" (110,000; 2 Zerg research; unlocks Hellion, Sensor Tower)
Primary: cleanse the infestation = destroy 144 infested structures. Day/night cycle: day ~3 min (move out), night ~5 min (defend), starts at day; HUD shows the remaining cycle time. Night 1: only Infested Terrans (~25 hp); halfway through night 1 a Factory + Hellions are granted. Night 2: Infested Marines join. Night 3: destructible debris at the back of the base falls and a third path opens. Infested attack/defence upgrades come periodically; Aberrations after several nights. Destroying a structure spawns broodlings; at night destroyed/damaged buildings spawn many units (they do not leave to attack). Bonus: kill 2 Infestors (+1 Zerg research each) — burrowed by day with a revealed sight circle; reveal with a Missile Turret. Achievements: finish before 5th night (Normal), before 4th night, 15 structures at night (Hard).
## 6a Safe Haven — "Protect the Haven colony" (125,000; 3 Zerg research; Viking)
Unlock: Outbreak + 2 later missions (Liquipedia prerequisite text says "Outbreak and 7 other missions" on the mission page; table says any 2). Primary: destroy 3 Protoss Nexuses; destroy the Purifier Mothership (only attackable after all 3 Nexuses fall; Vortex vs clumped units; reaches your main base last). Start: well-established base at top. Protoss attack your base twice early; Colossus attacks later; Terror Fleets (1: 2 Carriers; 2: 2 Carriers + 2 Void Rays + Scout; 3: 3-4 Void Rays + 2 Scouts) arrive as the Purifier destroys colonist outposts; each is a bonus objective; saving outposts drops resources. Nexus defences: Warp Prisms power cannons; Phoenixes, Colossus, Void Rays + High Templar guard. Achievements: save 3 outposts (Normal), 2 (Hard); 50 Viking kills.
## 6b Haven's Fall — "Destroy the Zerg infestation" (125,000; 3 Protoss research; Viking)
Primary: destroy the Zerg infestation. Start: west (9 o'clock), SCVs, 3 Vikings, 2 Firebats, 4 Marines. First infested base nearby has Brood Lords and no anti-air; destroying it stops attacks on your main for a while. Colonies to the south get infested by Virophages (a morphing building); save them (nuke/kill the Virophage before it finishes) or infested streams converge on you. Ultralisk guards the northern base. Achievements: 3 settlements protected (Normal), 5 (Hard); 50 structures by Vikings.
## 7 Smash and Grab — "Seize the Xel'Naga Artifact" (110,000; 4 Protoss research; Marauder: 100 hp, no stim until upgrades)
Primary: get to the artifact; destroy the 3 Stone Zealots (activate when you enter the artifact circle, like small Colossi; they do not pursue). Start: small force at the 7 o'clock position; Zerg attack from the top-left (drop pods + base), Protoss attack once at the start with 3 Stalkers. Zerg race: unless you kill the Zerg, the Zerg claim the artifact (time pressure). Zerg base: 1 Hive + 6 Hatcheries. Bonus: 4 Protoss relics (+1 Protoss research each): right outside your base; west (Spine/cannon/Stalker guarded); southwest of the Protoss ramp (3 cannons); in the alcove right before the artifact. Protoss base: High Templar (Brutal), Sentries, cannons, Void Ray. Achievements: no unit lost to Stone Guardians (Normal), < 15 min (Hard), all 4 relics w/o destroying zerg (Normal).
## 8 The Dig — "Recover the Xel'Naga Artifact" (120,000; 3 Protoss research; Siege Tank). Unlock: Smash and Grab + 7 other missions
Primary: recover the Laser Drill; destroy the Xel'Naga Temple Door; the Laser Drill must survive. Start: few Marines, 2 Medics, a Marauder; Protoss ambush on the way, Siege Tanks granted along the path; at the encampment you control all structures except the Laser Drill. You get the Drill after the first Archons appear; it can auto-fire at units (Colossus, Immortals priority) but dealing damage to the Door is the goal. Protoss attack intensity is tied to damage on the Door; when the Door is ~50% (guide: around 1000 hp left) the Protoss attack non-stop. Door destroyed = victory regardless of base. Waves: Zealots/Stalkers, then Immortals/Colossi/High Templar/Archons, air raids (Carrier+2 Void Rays on the second), Warp Prisms. Bonus: 3 Protoss relics (revealed halfway; housed in buildings that the Drill must destroy; need sight; a unit picks up the relic). 2 Nexus in northern bases rebuilt only twice. Achievements: 20 Drill kills (Normal), 50 Protoss structures (Hard), no Drill kills (Normal).
## 9 The Moebius Factor — "Evacuate the Moebius Foundation" (120,000; 3 Zerg research; Medivac). Unlock: The Dig + 11 other missions
Primary: destroy 3 Data Cores; Kerrigan must not access a data core (she destroys abandoned structures over time — "Hard Core": finish before she destroys 6 on Hard). Start: 3 o'clock small base, no units; soon granted 4 War Pigs, 4 Marines, 2 Medivacs. Cores: first is nearby on a high ground (Spine Crawlers; patrolling Corruptor), second has a Zerg base next to it (Nydus worms pop up when damaged), third is farthest (Nydus + Brood Lord + Ultralisks). Zerg attack your main during the 2nd core. Bonus: kill the Brutalisk (+3 Zerg research) on an island platform mid-map (undefended high ground NE of it); rescue 5 stranded Moebius groups (en route to core 1; west of base on a platform; helipad at core 2; a rooftop outside core 2; a building below it — hover an air unit to reveal). Achievements: all survivors (Normal), before 6 structures lost (Hard), only SCV/Medivac/merc training (Normal).
## 10 Supernova — "Seize the Xel'Naga Artifact" (120,000; 4 Protoss research; Banshee). Unlock: Moebius Factor + 14 total missions
Primary: clear the landing zone; destroy the Artifact Vault. Start: 4 Banshees near the south-west middle, base lands after the zone is cleared; resources scarce. A slow wall of fire advances (from one side) killing everything it touches; relocate by lifting off to the destroyed Protoss base east. Enemy base top-left warps in pylons/buildings after your Command Center lands; Protoss bases east and north-east (artifact base). Vault can be damaged by the fire but it only fails if the fire gets the last hit (so you need the last hit, per guide). Bonus: 4 Protoss relics (+1 each): outside base right (Zealot+Stalker); SE next to an expansion; NE; alcove south of the vault (2 Templar + cannons). Fire passes reveal relic locations. Achievements: no unit lost to the fire, 75 kills with cloaked Banshees (Hard), no Barracks/Factory units (Normal).
## 11 Maw of the Void — "Recover the Xel'Naga Artifact" (125,000; 4 Protoss research; Battlecruiser). Unlock: Supernova
Primary: clear the landing zone; destroy the Xel'Naga Vault (ends the mission). Start: 3 Battlecruisers; base lands after the zone is cleared. Rip-Field Generators: slowly damage everything in their area except shielded Protoss; only Battlecruisers survive long enough; Generators guarded by Photon Cannons. Protoss drop Archons/Stalkers/Zealots/Immortals onto your island after a few minutes; later Carriers (up to 5), Void Rays. Mothership warps in near the vault, can teleport and Vortex. Bonus: rescue 4 Protoss prisoners (each prison holds 3 Dark Templar; destroy the prison; a bridge forms) (+4 Protoss research total). Achievements: destroy all Rip-Field Generators (Normal); no unit lost in the Rip-Field (Hard); destroy all Protoss structures (Normal).
## 12 The Devil's Playground — "Gather 8000 Minerals" (110,000; 3 Zerg research; Reaper). Unlock: The Evacuation OR Smash and Grab (and total >= 4 missions)
Primary: mine 8000 minerals. Start: left-middle, a few structures + SCVs. Lava rises periodically (warning/timer; none on Brutal): SCVs must be on high ground; Command Centers can load 5 SCVs and lift. Bonus: locate Tosh's miners (right next to the start: a free Command Center + 5 SCVs; 26 Reaper crew members hidden around the map in all); kill the Brutalisk (1500 hp, 50 dmg, bottom-left; kite it into lava from the east ramp). Zerg attack from different directions; containers (minerals/gas) along the way and on destroyed zerg buildings. Achievements: kill the Brutalisk with lava (Normal), find all crew (Hard), kill Brutalisk without losing a unit (Normal), destroy all zerg structures (Normal).
## 13 Welcome to the Jungle — Gather Terrazine (7); Protoss can't seal 7 altars (120,000; 3 Protoss research; Goliath). Unlock: Devil's Playground
Primary: capture 7 canisters of terrazine (13 deposits on the map, guarded by Tal'darim "altars"); fail if the Protoss seal 7 altars (the 4 on the right side can be ignored); alternative win: kill everything Protoss. Starting base: bottom centre, small force; Protoss base upper-left, launches periodic attacks; extracting terrazine triggers an attack aimed at the SCV (7th triggers a huge force). Bonus: find 3 Protoss relics (north of base undefended; middle-left guarded by 3 Zealots; top-right past two altars, cannons + Stalkers). Protoss use many air units, Colossi, Storm. Achievements: no SCV killed (Normal); prevent any altar capped (Hard); < 14 min (Normal); destroy all Protoss structures.
## 14a Breakout (Tosh) — Destroy the Main Prison Base; Tosh must survive (125,000 +50,000 bonus)
Tosh is the only controlled unit (Spectre hero, permanently cloaked; detectors: turrets, Ravens, scans). Raynor sends allied waves along a path; each prison base captured becomes his outpost (waves gain Marauders/Medics). Spells in order: Mind Blast (50 energy, 35 dmg + 2 s stun, small AoE), Psi Shield (30 energy, absorbs 300 within 10 s), Consumption (drain a friendly bio for energy). Ghost EMP at the second area removes cloak/energy. Nukes: 4 available at the main facility (fast launch for Tosh). Bonus: kill guards at Cell Block A (25,000 credits; Diamondbacks join future waves) and Cell Block B (25,000; Siege Tanks join; Raven patrols with a window). Dominion bases: tanks, turrets, bunkers, Hellions, Thors, Ravens, mercenary Battlecruiser, Starport making Ravens. Achievements: Tosh never below 100 hp (Normal), < 25 min (Hard), 50 kills by nukes (Normal).
## 14b Ghost of a Chance (Nova) — Destroy Jorium Stockpile; Destroy Terrazine Tank; Destroy Psi-Indoctrinator; Nova must survive (125,000 +50,000)
Three sections; units from a section do not carry over. S1: Nova + 8 Marines + 2 Medics; gate control; Dominate a Siege Tank; 2 Spectres; Jorium Stockpile guarded by a Battlecruiser, "Hel's Angel", "Jackson's Revenge" and turrets. S2: Nova alone, dominate a Raven, then reinforcements land (8 Reapers, 2 Siege Tanks, a Raven); gate control; bunker; Banshee + Siege Tank on high ground; Spectre that nukes if non-Nova units are seen; nuke silo; Terrazine Tanks (nuke the gathered units). S3: Nova + 4 Vikings + 4 Banshees; dominate a Siege Tank; nuke silo north; Raven + 4 War Pigs + 2 Marauders block a strip; debris frees a dominable Ultralisk; a Thor; 2 Spectres; Psi-Indoctrinator. An unused section-2 nuke carries to section 3. Abilities: Snipe (cd 10 s, 125 dmg, biological only), Domination (75 energy; lasts until the unit dies or is released). Bonus: kill 10 Spectres (5000 credits each; 2 in S1, 3 in S2, 5 in S3). Achievements: 15 kills by dominated units (Normal), kill every enemy (Hard), 2 Spectres with Reapers.
## 15 The Great Train Robbery — Destroy 8 Dominion Trains; don't let 2 trains get away (110,000; 3 Zerg research; Diamondback). Unlock: 6 total missions
Primary: destroy 8 trains; fail if 2 get away on Brutal (3 on lower difficulties). Start: centre of the map, small force; 3 tracks. Income comes from salvage containers and train kills (limited minerals). Trains come in order with escorts that grow stronger; after train 4 the Dominion builds bunkers along the tracks (SCV builders can be killed); after train 5 two Marauder Kill Teams (~15 Marauders + 3 Medics each) roam clockwise around the base; trains 6 and 7 are speed-boosted (need ~8 Diamondbacks) with Hellion escort; train 8 has a stronger escort and ends the mission when destroyed. Enemy attacks your main around train 2. Bonus: find all 6 abandoned Diamondbacks (first 3 revealed; others: top-centre high ground, mid-map platform by the middle track, south-east high ground between the middle and bottom tracks); find 3 Defiler Bones (revealed by the 5-minute mark; 2 at the start, third revealed by train 2). Achievements: kill the Marauder Kill Team (Normal), no train passes (Hard), build no SCVs (Normal).
## 16 Cutthroat — Buy merc contract before Orlan (6000 minerals); Destroy Orlan's Mercenary Fortress/Planetary Fortress (120,000; 3 Protoss research; Vulture + Spider Mines)
Primary: gather 6000 minerals to hire Mira Han's mercenaries (Orlan is racing to the same 6000; destroying his 3 revealed Command Centers + SCVs slows him; he also mines from his main), then destroy Orlan's Planetary Fortress. You may skip the first by destroying the fortress before Orlan hits 6000. Start: 3 o'clock; SCVs, Marines; 4 Vultures granted shortly after the start. Limited minerals: income from salvage. Orlan's forces scattered, rarely attack; he nukes you near his base. Han's base gives you mercenary tanks and mineral fields. Bonus: find 3 Protoss relic contraband (giant white salvage: north of base, south-west, middle) (+1 Protoss each). Achievements: 25 spider mine kills; don't train extra SCVs before hiring Han (Hard); destroy the fortress before hiring Han (Normal).
## 17 Engine of Destruction — Escort Tychus to the Odin; Tychus must survive; Destroy all Dominion bases (5); Odin must survive (120,000; 3 Zerg research; Wraith)
Primary: escort Tychus to the Odin (bottom-left start, small Marine/Medic force, no real resistance); then Tychus pilots the Odin through 5 Dominion bases while you hold a base. The Dominion never attacks your base while the Odin is moving; once it rests, base attacks come (Vultures after base 2, Reapers after base 3); base 3 mixed; base 4 has tanks on high ground; base 5 has several Battlecruisers; after base 5's units die the Odin launches a nuke, then the mission ends. Bonus: find and destroy the Loki (hero Battlecruiser, 2000 hp, Yamato; north side of base 4); find 3 Devourer tissue samples (the starting lab, base 2, base 4). Achievements: kill Loki (Normal), Odin never below 30% (Hard), repair < 5000 damage on the Odin (Normal).
## 18 Media Blitz — Destroy Dominion during the Surprise Attack; Access Broadcast Tower 1/2/3 (120,000; no research; Thor)
Primary: you start as the Odin in the middle of the map, surrounded by Dominion forces who turn on you when you fire: a timed Surprise Attack phase (~?) in which you destroy as much as you can (production structures matter); the Odin then appears in your base when the timer runs out and you build economy. Three Dominion bases: left (air: 4 Starports, Barracks, Factory), top (Factory/tanks), right (infantry). Then upload to each of 3 broadcast towers (clockwise); while uploading you are attacked by the two unit types not in that base; Ravens/Medivacs appear mid-upload. Ending: all three uploads done. Secret: destroying the Science Facility in the lower-right corner yields documents that unlock Piercing the Shroud (must be done within a full completion of the mission). Achievements: barracks+factory+starport during the sneak attack (Normal); < 20 min (Hard); 120 kills in the sneak attack (Normal).
## 19 Piercing the Shroud (secret) — Investigate the Dominion Lab; Destroy the Fusion Reactor/Generator; Escape From The Facility; Raynor must survive (125,000; 3 Zerg + 4 Protoss research). Unlock: Media Blitz incl. secret objective
Dungeon crawler: Raynor + 5 Marines + 2 Medics; Raynor picks up weapons (grenades, Plasma Rifle, research pickups, Chrono Rift (slows the Hybrid)). Part 1: labs, a console to choose reinforcements — 70 Zerglings, 20 Zealots or 5 Ultralisks (before lab 2); an ARES (anti-armor missiles) console; Hel's Angel, Siege Breaker, tanks, a Thor (Thor can one-shot Raynor on higher difficulty); reinforcement console choice (8 Marines, 4 Firebats, 3 Marauders, or 3 Marines + 3 Medics); optional Brutalisk room; two Perdition Turrets at the Fusion Generator room; destroying it starts part 2: Escape: the Hybrid chases and is invulnerable (run; it disappears after a while and reappears near the end); Pygmalisk eggs + an Ultralisk; reach the transport. Bonus: 4 Protoss relics (holding cell in lab 2; Brutalisk room; two during the escape); kill the Brutalisk (research). Achievements: kill Brutalisk with no losses (Normal), find all 17 weapon pickups (Hard), 40 kills with pickups (Normal).
## 20 Whispers of Doom (Zeratul) — Bring Zeratul to each Xel'Naga Shrine (3); Zeratul must survive; Escape to the Void Seeker (no credits; 3 Protoss + 3 Zerg research; Stalker)
Dungeon crawler: Zeratul alone, then 4 Stalkers (replenished to 5 at a checkpoint). Zeratul's Void Prison freezes detectors. Zerg attack at the scourge area: a few Mutalisks, 4 Roaches, an Ultralisk; Banelings gathered; Karass (High Templar hero) + Zealots arrive and charge; Nydus worms appear (destroy them); third shrine triggers a cinematic and the objective to escape to the Void Seeker — a pursuing swarm; rocks along the route slow the swarm if left intact; reaching the Void Seeker = victory. Bonus: destroy 3 Zerg hatcheries (+1 Zerg research each): middle (3 Mutalisks, 2 Overseers, Broodlord, 3 Spines); bottom-left (Overseer, spine+spore, 2 burrowed Ultralisks); near the third shrine (Roaches, spine, 2 spores). Achievements: >=3 Stalkers (Normal), Zeratul takes no life damage (Hard), third shrine with no Stalker loss.
## 21 A Sinister Turn (Zeratul line 2) — Power-up the Abandoned Base (6); Free 3 Preservers (research) (no credits; 3+3 research; Probe/Zealot/Immortal/High Templar/Dark Templar)
Base-building as Protoss: power the 6 unpowered buildings with Pylons; free 3 Preservers from stasis prisons (1500 hp each); Maar (hybrid) attacks the base periodically (800/800 first, 1000/1000 second, 1400/1400 afterwards; later casts a levitation spell, blink and a building-destroying spell; respawns when you kill a stasis prison); two Zhakul side bases (east holds the Templar Archives; southwest has High Templar) and a main Zhakul base with Void Rays and Carriers. Bonus: power up 3 Protoss buildings (Robotics Facility -> Immortal; Dark Shrine -> 2 Dark Templar; Templar Archives). Achievements: kill all Protoss (Normal), < 25 min (Hard), 30 kills with Dark Templar (Normal).
## 22 Echoes of the Future (Zeratul line 3) — Reach the Nexus; Bring Zeratul to the Overmind Tendrils (4) (research); Zeratul must survive (no credits; 2 Protoss + 4 Zerg research; Observer, Colossus)
Zeratul alone (no detection on the way) reaches the Nexus, which starts a timer and escalating Zerg waves at your base (Roach/Ultralisk later; Nydus worms can be Void-Prisoned). Four Overmind Tendrils: NE of base (also unlocks a Warpgate and gives free units if powered), right-most (Infested Terrans guard), two together to the north; reaching the final tendril ends the mission. Bonus: power 2 Obelisks (+1 Protoss each): NE next to the nearest tendril; north-northwest by the pointed-out Warpgate. Achievements: 50 kills by Zeratul (Normal), < 20 min (Hard), 2 tendrils within 4 min (Normal).
## 23 In Utter Darkness (Zeratul line 4) — Kill 2500 enemy forces (research); Defend until the last Protoss falls (no credits; 3 Protoss + 3 Zerg research; Phoenix, Void Ray, Carrier, Mothership)
Primary: kill 2500 units (Hard 2000, Normal 1500); defend until the last Protoss falls (mission continues after the kill goal; a huge Corruptor swarm ends it around the 39-40 minute mark, wave 69). Start: well-established Protoss base with a gold expansion; waves from 2 sides then more. Heroes granted at 5 points: Urun + Phoenixes, Mohandar + Void Rays, Selendis + Carriers, Artanis in a Mothership (ground only). Nydus worms pop up in base. Hybrids (levitate them with Phoenix/Mothership). Bonus: protect the Protoss Archive for 25:00 (the Archive submerges; a large attack comes ~30 s before). Achievements: +250 kills (Normal), +750 kills (Normal), no Stargate units (Normal).
## 24 Gates of Hell — Gather a Large Army (100 supply); Destroy the Nydus Worms (3) (no credits or research)
First Valerian mission; after starting it, unplayed non-final missions become unavailable. Start: base in the map's middle surrounded by Zerg; defence force with bunkers and Siege Tanks, limited gas. Part 1: Dominion drop pods land across the map (guarded by spontaneously-spawning Zerg): 10 pods; the last 4 fall 2 at a time (7th Banshees/Hellions, 8th Vikings, 9th Battlecruisers, 10th Thors); rescue units to reach 100 supply (the rescued pods count). Zerg attack your base from both sides. Part 2: destroy the 3 Nydus Worms near General Warfield's crash site on the right (Broodlords, Banelings, Ultralisks defend; a northern Zerg base can attack). 6 Spore Cannons on the map. Achievements: destroy all spore cannons (Normal); rescue 10 drop pods (Hard); rescue Warfield with restricted training.
## 25a Belly of the Beast — Plant Seismic Charges at each Fissure (3); Kill the Queen; Run to the Extraction Point/Escape the cave (no credits)
Dungeon crawler, 4 heroes: Raynor (Penetrator Round: 300 dmg to all units in a line), Tychus (Grenade: 200 dmg in target area), Swann (Flaming Betty: placeable flame turret), Stetmann (Regeneration of life+energy when idle). A hero at 0 hp is incapacitated (revived by another hero) except during the final escape where they die (mission fail). 3 caverns: each has a fissure (a charge with 1000 hp that you protect while it arms; two Ultralisks come when the timer nears 0). Cavern 1 ambush at the bottom-right; 4 Nydus worms. Cavern 2: 25-hp Infested Terrans, spine crawlers, Aberration and Banelings, Dominion troops to save. Cavern 3: eggs, Omegalisk breaks rocks to attack the last Dominion force; then the Queen (4000 hp, burrows and reappears at egg groups, Mind Blast stuns heroes). Escape: lava rises; reach the beacon; final Ultralisk. Achievements: no hero incapacitated (Normal); 50 kills with one Penetrator Round (Hard); 100 kills by Flaming Betty (Normal).
## 25b Shatter the Sky — Destroy the Coolant/Cooling Towers (4); bonus: kill the Zerg Leviathan (no credits)
Start: top-right, decent army and economy. 4 zerg platforms each with a Cooling Tower; destroying one gives 45 s to evacuate the platform before it explodes (the 4th tower ends the mission immediately). First tower: south of start behind an expansion with an Ultralisk. After the 2nd tower the Leviathan (3000 hp, range 12, spawns Mutalisks and Scourge, Fungal-like spell) appears and patrols the centre (bonus: destroy it; achievement: within 2 minutes of appearing). Remaining towers: southeast, northwest, southwest (strongest). Achievements: no unit lost to a platform explosion (Normal), < 25 min (Hard), Leviathan in 2 min (Normal).
## 26 All-In — Defend the Artifact (no credits)
Final mission. Two variants: after Belly of the Beast you face Zerg AIR (Leviathan 3000 hp at ~70% charge, Mutalisks/Broodlords); after Shatter the Sky you face NYDUS worms (300/400/500 hp by difficulty, spawn stronger units the longer they live). Start: base with 2 ground chokepoints + artifact cliff, Merc Compound. The Artifact charges for ~3 minutes before its Energy Nova (kills all Zerg in a huge radius except Kerrigan/Leviathan, which take damage; nova costs ~12.5% charge in the guide's numbering). A charge timer shows the % progress; mission ends when the Artifact is fully charged. At ~42% an Overlord drop wave. Kerrigan appears several times (1000/1250/1500 hp, 3 armor, detector, Razor Swarm + Implosion) and a final time near 100%. Achievements: 150 kills with the Artifact (Normal), use the Artifact only once (Hard), never use it (Normal).
