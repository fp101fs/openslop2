# GAME DESIGN PLAN & ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION
# Project: Future Humanity - 3-Faction RTS (SC2 Engine)

================================================================================
1. CORE PREMISE & WORLD BUILDING
================================================================================

Essentially StarCraft II mechanics, but instead of alien species, three competing 
branches of future humanity.

The factions are technologically and visually distinct, not simply modern countries 
with futuristic weapons. They are fictional successor civilizations inspired by 
those historical traditions rather than simplistic national stereotypes.

CENTRAL PREMISE:
Humanity has successfully colonized the solar system. Earth and the interplanetary 
colonies have fractured into three competing civilizations, and all three fiercely 
believe they represent humanity's rightful evolutionary and societal future.

================================================================================
2. THE THREE FACTIONS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FACTION 1: RED — THE EAST (The Ascendant Collective)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept: Collective / Technocratic civilization.
Inspiration: East Asia-inspired future technocracy.
Focus: Mass production, AI coordination, robotics, automated swarms, precision 
manufacturing, and networked machine intelligence.

Core Capabilities & Themes:
- Autonomous combat drones & airborne swarm matrices
- Heavy bipedal and quadrupedal walking mechs
- Nanotechnology rapid repair and automated fabrication field units
- Long-range hyper-velocity railgun artillery
- Cybernetically linked, AI-coordinated drone infantry

Gameplay Identity:
- Numbers, automation, overwhelming scalable production, synchronized swarm tactics.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FACTION 2: BLUE — THE WEST (The Atlantic Federation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept: Federation / Military-industrial civilization.
Inspiration: North America, Europe, Australia-inspired defense-industrial complex.
Focus: Advanced military engineering, individual combat superiority, aerospace 
mastery, directional energy weapons, and high-tier defensive shielding.

Core Capabilities & Themes:
- Elite powered exoskeleton infantry (CMC Marines, Juggernauts)
- Advanced aerospace fighters and active-camouflage stealth aircraft
- Tactical localized nuclear and plasma strike ordnance
- Regenerative kinetic and energy deflection shields
- Heavy modular combat vehicles, siege tanks, and dropships

Gameplay Identity:
- Elite units, immense focused firepower, tactical mobility, survivability, and fortification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FACTION 3: PURPLE — THE OUTLANDS (The Transhuman Diaspora)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept: Independent / Transhuman civilization.
Inspiration: Africa, Middle East, South America, India, and outer rim frontiers.
Focus: Decentralized command, radical experimentation, synthetic biology, 
accelerated genetic engineering, nanite swarms, and asymmetrical warfare.

Core Capabilities & Themes:
- Bioengineered infantry and synthetic bio-soldiers
- Living, self-mutating combat organisms and bio-structures
- Nanite clouds capable of disabling, corroding, and consuming enemy mechanical hulls
- Adaptive reactive camouflage and cloaked ambush organisms
- Genetic super-soldiers with rapid cellular regeneration

Gameplay Identity:
- Dynamic adaptation, tactical disruption, debuffs, shape-shifting, and unconventional warfare.

4. KEY FUTURISTIC UNIT ROSTER (EXAMPLES & CONCEPTS)
================================================================================

[RED - The East]
- Swarm Carrier: Massive command platform that deploys and coordinates dozens of 
  miniature lethal attack drones simultaneously.
- Railgun Tank: Heavy armored mobile chassis with extreme range and devastating 
  line-penetration projectile weaponry.
- Automated Combat Drone: Cheap, rapid-fabrication autonomous skirmisher unit.
- Nanite Fabricator: Mobile nanotech repair and assembly walker.

[BLUE - The West]
- Juggernaut: Slow, heavily armored super-infantry equipped with dual rotary 
  autocannons and ballistic plating (280 HP, 3 Armor).
- Phantom: High-tech aerospace fighter with active thermal stealth, reconnaissance, 
  and targeted assassination munitions.
- Powered Marine: Frontline combat infantry with Gauss kinetic rifles and 
  stimpack combat stimulants.
- Siege Platform: Mobile multi-role artillery system with deployable shock cannons.

[PURPLE - The Outlands]
- Shifter: Morphogenetic combat unit capable of dynamically altering its physical 
  morphology in real time to counter specific enemy armor and unit classes.
- Nanite Cloud: Semi-sentient aerosol cloud that corrodes, disables, and 
  gradually consumes enemy machinery and structures while healing bio-allies.
- Bio-Stalker: Agile genetic predator with cloaking and neurotoxin spit.
- Regenerative Brood-Tank: Living organic assault beast that regenerates rapidly 
  out of combat.

5. TACTICAL AI & BEHAVIOR SUBROUTINE UPGRADE SYSTEM (PROGRAMMABLE BEHAVIORS)
================================================================================

A central innovative mechanic allowing commanders to customize and upgrade 
behavioral routines ("AI logic scripts") for individual units, entire unit types, 
or the army as a whole.

A. Scope Levels:
1. Individual Unit: Select a specific veteran unit or commander unit and assign 
   tailored behavioral rules.
2. Unit Class / Type: Configure doctrine for all Marines, all Juggernauts, 
   all Swarm Drones, etc.
3. Global Army: Set civilization-wide stance and target acquisition doctrine.

B. Upgradeable Behavioral Modules / Routine Trees:
- Target Prioritization Matrix:
  * Prioritize low-health enemies (focus fire to eliminate DPS)
  * Prioritize armored / high-threat siege units
  * Prioritize enemy repair / spellcaster units (e.g., Medivacs / Nanite Fabricators)
  * Target nearest threat (default)

- Engagement & Kiting Doctrine:
  * Dynamic Stutter-Step / Hit-and-Run: Automatically fire and step back against 
    slower melee or short-range units.
  * Hold Fire / Ambush Stance: Stay silent and cloaked until high-value targets 
    enter optimal kill zones.
  * Bodyguard / Escort Protocol: Automatically orbit and protect vulnerable siege 
    platforms or transport units.

- Tactical Self-Preservation:
  * Tactical Retreat Threshold: Unit automatically breaks off and retreats to base 
    or friendly repair aura when falling below 25% HP.
  * Stimpack / Ability Trigger Rule: Automatically inject combat stims when 
    engaging 3+ hostiles, but never below 30 HP.
  * Nanite / Shield Overcharge Routine: Automatically pop emergency defense matrix 
    when taking heavy burst damage.

C. Progression & Economy:
- Purchased through dedicated research facilities (e.g., Red "Neural Core", 
  Blue "Tactical Command Matrix", Purple "Synaptic Nexus").
- Unlocks both automated routine slots and reactive behavioral behaviors that 
  reward tactical macro and micro play.

================================================================================
6. ENGINE & MULTI-VIEW ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
- RTS View: Classic isometric strategic command, drag-box marquee, control groups, 
  fog of war, minimap, macro production, and base building.
- First-Person View (FPS Mode): Direct unit cockpit/visor zoom, mouse look, WASD 
  locomotion, and manual weapon firing with high-visibility projectiles.
- Third-Person View (TPS Mode): Over-the-shoulder action perspective keeping full 
  unit 3D model visible in action with pinch zoom follow distance.
- Hardware Target: Metal GPU acceleration on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) + 
  DirectX 12/Vulkan on Windows via modern high-performance WebGL2 pipeline.
